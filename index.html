THE PALACE PHILOSOPHY

Where Five Centuries of Craft Meet Still Water

Arriving by private wooden shikara across Lake Pichola, guests step through hand-carved torana gateways into quiet colonnades scented with night-blooming jasmine and vetiver.

Every jharokha balcony, hand-painted Mewari fresco, and inlaid white Makrana marble floor was sculpted over seven years by master sompura artisans of Rajasthan. With only 42 suites and private pavilions across nine acres of terraced lakefront gardens, tranquility is never compromised.
